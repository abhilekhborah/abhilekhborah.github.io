About Me

I am currently a Member of Technical Staff (ML) at Adobe, where I work on evaluations and personalized experiences for users interacting with conversational AI through Adobe Brand Concierge. Previously, I was a Research Intern at Microsoft Research, India, working with Vineeth N. Balasubramanian and Ravishankar Krishnaswamy on pre-training multimodal embedding models for visual document retrieval. I also spent a fall at the Centre for Trusted Internet Community, National University of Singapore, where I worked under Prof. Kokil Jaidka. I have also had long-term research collaborations at the AI Institute, University of South Carolina, where I worked under Prof. Amit Sheth. I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering from Manipal University Jaipur, India (2022-2026).

My research is about building human-centered AI systems that align with human intent and values, capture diverse human behavior across social and cultural contexts, and remain reliable and safe as they learn, adapt, and interact with their environments. Below, I outline the research directions I pursue, their impact, and the work done around them.

Publications

ICML 2026 Workshop

Bosses, Kings, and the Commons: Cooperation Under Power Asymmetry in LLM Societies

Abhilekh Borah

Pluralistic Alignment Workshop, International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026

Paper

A simulation in which LLM agents with different levels of authority repeatedly decide how much to extract from a shared resource, to study how unequal power shapes cooperation and collective behavior in multi agent systems.

LREC 2026

ObfusQAte: A Proposed Framework to Evaluate LLM Robustness on Obfuscated Factual Question Answering

Shubhra Ghosh*, Abhilekh Borah*, Aditya Kumar Guru*, Kripabandhu Ghosh

Language Resources and Evaluation Conference (LREC), 2026

Paper Code

Tests whether LLMs truly understand the meaning of factual questions or rely on patterns memorized during training, by transforming questions into semantically equivalent forms that keep the answer the same.

ICLR 2026

Social Agents: Collective Intelligence Improves LLM Predictions

Aanisha Bhattacharyya*, Abhilekh Borah*, Yaman Kumar Singla*, Rajiv Ratn Shah, Changyou Chen, Balaji Krishnamurthy

International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR), 2026

Paper Project Page

Inspired by the wisdom of the crowd, Social Agents creates personas with different demographic and psychographic attributes that independently judge content (like advertisements, videos, and webpages), and combines their opinions to predict how real people respond across eleven behavioral tasks.

EACL 2026 Main

Don't Judge a Book by its Cover: Testing LLMs' Robustness Under Logical Obfuscation (Oral)

Abhilekh Borah, Shubhra Ghosh*, Kedar Joshi*, Aditya Kumar Guru*, Kripabandhu Ghosh

Conference of the European Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (EACL), 2026

Paper Code Dataset

Tests whether LLMs truly understand the underlying structure of a question or rely on patterns memorized during training, by transforming questions into logically equivalent forms that keep the answer the same.

AAAI 2026 AIA Track

DETONATE - A Benchmark for Text-to-Image Alignment and Kernelized Direct Preference Optimization (Oral)

Renjith Prasad*, Abhilekh Borah*, Hasnat Md Abdullah*, Chathurangi Shyalika, Gurpreet Singh, Ritvik Garimella, Rajarshi Roy, Harshul Surana, Nasrin Imanpour, Suranjana Trivedy, Amit Sheth, Amitava Das

AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2026, Special Track on AI Alignment (AIA)

Paper Project Page Code

A large-scale benchmark of human-verified hateful and non-hateful image pairs across race, gender, and disability, along with DPO-Kernels, a preference-learning method for teaching text-to-image models human safety preferences.

AAAI 2026 Student Abstract

Always Refuse: Steering LLMs Against Jailbreaks with Contrastive Activations

Abhilekh Borah, Niranjan Chebrolu, Kokil Jaidka

AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2026, Student Abstract and Poster Program

Paper Code

A training-free method that compares model activations for safe and unsafe requests to find a refusal direction, then applies it during inference to help models refuse harmful requests under jailbreak attacks.

IJCNLP-AACL 2025 Main

The Visual Counter Turing Test (VCT2): A Benchmark for Evaluating AI-Generated Image Detection and the Visual AI Index (VAI)

Nasrin Imanpour*, Abhilekh Borah*, Shashwat Bajpai*, Subhankar Ghosh*, Sainath Reddy Sankepally*, Hasnat Md Abdullah, Nishoak Kosaraju, Shreyas Dixit, Ashhar Aziz, Shwetangshu Biswas, Vinija Jain, Aman Chadha, Song Wang, Amit Sheth, Amitava Das

International Joint Conference on Natural Language Processing and Conference of the Asia-Pacific Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (IJCNLP-AACL), 2025

Paper Code Dataset (COCO) Dataset (Twitter)

A benchmark of real and AI-generated images to evaluate AI-generated image detection methods, along with a metric to quantify and rank the perceptual realism of generated images.

EMNLP 2025 Main

Alignment Quality Index (AQI) - Beyond Refusals: AQI as an Intrinsic Alignment Diagnostic via Latent Geometry, Cluster Divergence, and Layer wise Pooled Representations (Oral)

Abhilekh Borah*, Chhavi Sharma*, Danush Khanna*, Utkarsh Bhatt, Gurpreet Singh, Hasnat Md Abdullah, Raghav Kaushik Ravi, Vinija Jain, Shubham Singh, Vasu Sharma, Arpita Vats, Rahul Raja, Aman Chadha, Amitava Das

Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2025

Paper Code Dataset (LITMUS)

An alignment metric that looks inside the model, measuring how distinctly safe and unsafe behaviors are represented in its activations, to assess alignment beyond output-based measures like refusal rate.

EMNLP 2025 Main

DRISHTIKON: A Multimodal Multilingual Benchmark for Testing Language Models' Understanding on Indian Culture

Arijit Maji, Raghvendra Kumar, Akash Ghosh, Anushka, Nemil Shah, Abhilekh Borah, Vanshika Shah, Nishant Mishra, Sriparna Saha

Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2025

Paper Code Dataset

A multimodal, multilingual benchmark testing whether vision-language models understand Indian culture (e.g., festivals, attire, cuisine, art forms, and heritage) across 15 languages, all 28 states, and 8 union territories.

ACL 2025 Workshop

CliME: Evaluating Multimodal Climate Discourse on Social Media and the Climate Alignment Quotient (CAQ) (🏆 Outstanding Paper Award)

Abhilekh Borah*, Hasnat Md Abdullah*, Kangda Wei, Ruihong Huang

Workshop on NLP for Positive Impact (NLP4PI), Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL), 2025

Paper Code Dataset

A multimodal dataset of climate-related social-media posts (primarily memes), with a metric that checks whether model responses are supported by evidence, relevant to the climate claim, and clearly communicated.
